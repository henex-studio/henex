Audit Task Rules
Before starting any audit, produce a plan covering scope, batches, estimated time per batch, and checklist items. Wait for confirmation before proceeding.
Before modifying any shared file, read all files that may be affected. State the full impact list first. Do not proceed until the impact scope is clear.
When referencing specific values from files, re-read the source. Do not rely on memory.
After each modification, provide a brief change record: filename, what changed, and why.
At the end of each audit batch, produce a short summary: what was fixed, what was deferred, and what the next batch covers.
