Philosophy

A framework distilled from practice.

HENEX is my working framework for reading and shaping design relationships across services, digital products and built environments.

It helps me move from observation to method, and from method to work that can be tested, delivered and used.

Explore the framework View Works

(Human + Emotion + Nature) × Nexus = Experience

Core idea

Design as relational work.

HENEX helps me read how relationships work, locate where relationships need support, and reorganise them into more coherent, meaningful and usable experiences.

Services

People may need a clearer path, stronger trust or better support to move forward.

Digital products

Interfaces may need better feedback, guidance and structure for confident action.

Built environments

Movement, material and context may need to work together to support public experience.

Framework structure

Three dimensions of every design relationship.

HENEX reads design relationships through three dimensions: Human, Emotion and Nature. The three dimensions work together to describe who is involved, what is felt, and where the experience happens.

Human

People within a design relationship.

Identity, perception and agency describe who is involved, how people understand and how people act.

  • Identity
  • Perception
  • Agency

Emotion

The felt quality of experience.

Affect, empathy and meaning describe what is felt, where support is needed and what gives the experience value.

  • Affect
  • Empathy
  • Meaning

Nature

The carrier where experience happens.

Medium, matter and interface describe what carries the relationship and where interaction takes place.

  • Medium
  • Matter
  • Interface

Nodes

Nine nodes for locating relational needs.

Each node points to a place where a design relationship may need attention. A node helps locate what needs to be understood, supported or reorganised.

Human node

Identity

Culture, role, belonging and self understanding.

Human node

Perception

How people read, sense and understand information or environments.

Human node

Agency

The ability to choose, act and influence outcomes.

Emotion node

Affect

The immediate tone or felt quality of an experience.

Emotion node

Empathy

Recognition of context, needs and support.

Emotion node

Meaning

Memory, value, story and purpose.

Nature node

Medium

The physical or digital carrier where experience happens.

Nature node

Matter

Material, form, texture, durability and physical quality.

Nature node

Interface

The touchpoint, page, screen or boundary where people interact with a system.

Method logic

From node relationships to recipes.

A recipe brings three nodes together, one from each dimension, to describe a fuller design situation. It is a practical method for reading and reorganising a specific relationship through design.
